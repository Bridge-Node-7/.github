# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Bridge Node 7 projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions and viewpoints
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing accountability and learning from mistakes

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately via the security contact described in `SECURITY.md` of the relevant repository (or the Bridge-Node-7 profile `SECURITY.md`). Maintainers will review and take appropriate action.
